NotchTimer

A Pomodoro timer that lives in your MacBook's notch.

Free on the Mac App Store. No account, no sync, no network access, no data collection.

What It Does

Start a session and a slim pill grows out of your MacBook's notch with a colour-coded countdown — a red flame while you work, a green cup on a short break, a blue leaf on a long one. On Macs without a notch it floats just below the menu bar instead.
