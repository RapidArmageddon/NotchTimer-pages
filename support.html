NotchTimer

Support

NotchTimer is a Pomodoro timer that lives in your MacBook's notch. Free, no account, no network.

Getting Started

NotchTimer runs in the menu bar and has no Dock icon and no windows. After launching, look for the flame icon in the menu bar toward the right of the screen. Click it and choose Start — a pill appears at the top centre of the screen with a countdown.

Using the Timer

  • Pause or resume: click the pill itself.
  • Skip a phase: menu bar → Skip Phase.
  • Stop: menu bar → Reset Timer (⌘R). This hides the pill.
  • Change durations: the Work, Short Break and Long Break submenus.
  • Show more or less: toggle Show Session Count and Show Progress Bar.
  • Auto-advance: on, the cycle flows straight from work into break. Off, each phase waits for you.

Colours

A red flame means a focus block, a green cup a short break, and a blue leaf a long break.

Macs Without a Notch

On an iMac, Mac mini, Mac Studio or any external display, the timer floats just below the menu bar at the top of the screen instead of growing out of the notch. Everything else works the same.

Common Questions

I can't see the timer. The pill only appears while a session is running. Start one from the menu bar.

Does it survive sleep? Yes. Close the lid mid-session and the timer picks the time back up correctly when you reopen it.

Is there a Dock icon? No, by design. NotchTimer lives only in the menu bar.
