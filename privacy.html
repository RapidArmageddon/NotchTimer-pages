NotchTimer

Privacy Policy — Last updated 7 November 2026

The short version: NotchTimer does not collect, store, or transmit any of your data. Everything stays on your Mac.

Data Collection

NotchTimer does not collect any personal data, usage analytics, crash reports, or telemetry of any kind. The app has no network access and makes no internet connections.

What Is Stored

NotchTimer stores only your own preferences — work and break durations, sounds, auto-advance, and whether the session counter and progress bar are shown. These are kept in macOS preferences on your Mac. There is no account, no sync, and no server. You can remove this data at any time by deleting the app.

Permissions

NotchTimer requires no special permissions. It does not access your:

  • Microphone or camera
  • Contacts, calendar, or photos
  • Location or network
  • Files of any kind

The app runs sandboxed with no additional entitlements.

Third-Party Services

NotchTimer uses no third-party services, SDKs, analytics frameworks, or advertising networks. It has no dependencies beyond macOS itself.

Children's Privacy

NotchTimer does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes

If this policy is ever updated, the changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.
